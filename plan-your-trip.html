Travel Essentials

Basic information to help visitors prepare before traveling around Tabasco.

🎒

What to Pack

Bring light clothing, sunscreen, sunglasses, insect repellent, comfortable shoes, a hat, and an umbrella for sun or rain.

💵

Currency

The local currency is the Mexican peso. Cards are accepted in many hotels and restaurants, but cash is recommended for small businesses.

🧾

Budget

Some attractions, food stands, local transportation, and extra activities may require additional money. Plan ahead and carry cash.

FAQs

Quick answers for visitors planning their trip to Tabasco.

What should I wear in Tabasco?

Light, comfortable clothes and breathable fabrics are recommended because the weather is hot and humid.

Can I drink tap water?

It is better to drink bottled or purified water during your trip.

Is it safe to visit natural attractions?

Yes, but visitors should follow local guides, stay with their group, and avoid swimming in unknown areas.

Do I need insect repellent?

Yes. It is especially useful in parks, rivers, jungles, natural areas, and during the evening.

What is the weather like in Tabasco?

Tabasco has a hot and humid tropical climate, so visitors should wear light clothing and stay hydrated.

What should I pack for Tabasco?

Light clothing, sunscreen, sunglasses, insect repellent and comfortable shoes are recommended because of the hot and humid weather.

What food should visitors try in Tabasco?

Tourists should try traditional dishes such as pejelagarto, tamales and Tabasco-style chocolate drinks.

How can I travel to Tabasco from World Cup host cities?

Visitors can travel to Tabasco by plane or bus from major host cities such as Mexico City, Monterrey and Guadalajara.

Is transportation available for tourists?

Yes. Visitors can use taxis, buses and transportation apps in many areas.

What archaeological or ecotourism sites can I visit in Tabasco?

You can visit Comalcalco, La Venta, Pantanos de Centla, and Agua Selva.

Are there flood problems in certain times of the year?

Yes, from October to November. Check the weather before you travel.

What health precautions should I take because of the hot weather?

Use repellent, drink water, avoid sun from 11 am to 3 pm, and wear light long sleeves.

Are there days or times when shops or museums close?

Many museums close on Mondays. Small shops close on Sunday afternoons.

What documents do I need if I travel from another country?

Valid passport. If your country does not need a visa for Mexico, you fill out the FMM form.

What is the local currency? Do they accept cards or US dollars?

Mexican peso (MXN). Cards are accepted in big hotels and restaurants. US dollars are not commonly accepted. Bring cash.

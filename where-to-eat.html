Pejelagarto Asado

The ultimate signature dish of Tabasco. A prehistoric freshwater fish grilled slowly over open charcoal embers, served with traditional chimichurri salsa, lime, and fresh tortillas.

Delicate tamales made from strained corn dough mixed with chipilín leaves (a wild regional herb). Filled with shredded chicken or cheese and topped with a mild tomato salsa.

Chanchamitos

Small, round tamales wrapped in dry corn husks (totomoxtle) and tied securely at both ends. The dough is seasoned with achiote and lard, filled with slow-cooked pork or chicken.

Tips for the Culinary Traveler

Make the most of your culinary adventure through the flavors of Tabasco.

🌶️
Respect the Amashito

The tiny amashito chili is a staple on every Tabasco table. It looks small and harmless, but it packs a powerful heat. Test a tiny drop first!

🧉
Unsweetened Pozol

Authentic pozol is traditionally unsweetened. It is consumed cold to keep you hydrated in the humid weather. Ask for sugar if you prefer a sweeter drink.

🐟
Fresh Catch Palapas

For the best pejelagarto and seafood, head to the coastal palapas in Paraíso or Frontera, where dishes are prepared right next to the river banks.

🍫
Cacao Hacienda Tours

Comalcalco is the heart of Mexican cacao. Take a guided tour of a cacao hacienda to taste raw cacao seeds fresh from the pod and learn chocolate history.

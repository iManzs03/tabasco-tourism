Pejelagarto Asado

The ultimate signature dish of Tabasco. A prehistoric freshwater fish grilled slowly over open charcoal embers, served with traditional chimichurri salsa, lime, and fresh tortillas.

🔥 Charcoal Grilled 🐟 River Fish 👑 Iconic
📍 Rivers & Lagoons of Tabasco

Tamales de Chipilín

Delicate tamales made from strained corn dough mixed with chipilín leaves (a wild regional herb). Filled with shredded chicken or cheese and topped with a mild tomato salsa.

🌿 Wild Herbs 🌽 Nixtamalized Corn 🍽️ Traditional
📍 Statewide

Chanchamitos

Small, round tamales wrapped in dry corn husks (totomoxtle) and tied securely at both ends. The dough is seasoned with achiote and lard, filled with slow-cooked pork or chicken.

🥓 Lard Seasoned 🍂 In Totomoxtle 🍖 Pork / Chicken
📍 Chontalpa Region
Dish Foto: El Gourmet

Puchero Tabasqueño

A hearty and comforting beef broth seasoned with local spices, showcasing regional root vegetables and fruits such as cassava (yuca), macal, sweet potato, and plantain.

🍲 Warm Broth 🍠 Local Tubers 🥩 Beef Stew
📍 Home Kitchens

Butifarra de Jalpa

An artisanal, highly popular pork sausage seasoned with black pepper, nutmeg, and regional spices. Served hot alongside shredded cabbage, lime, and fiery amashito pepper.

🍖 Pork Sausage 🌶️ Amashito Chili 🛠️ Artisanal
📍 Jalpa de Méndez
Dish Foto: SaboresdeMéxico

Piguas

Exquisite giant freshwater prawns harvested from local rivers, prized as a luxury delicacy. Best prepared with garlic butter (al mojo de ajo) or grilled over charcoal.

🦐 River Prawns 🧄 Garlic Butter ✨ Premium
📍 Paraíso & Frontera Coast
Drink Foto: PoderConsumidor

Pozol con Cacao

The ultimate pre-Hispanic ancestral drink. Made by whisking nixtamalized corn dough and locally harvested cacao paste in cold water. Incredibly refreshing and filling.

🍫 Organic Cacao ❄️ Ice Cold 🧉 Served in Jícara
📍 Ancestral Heritage
Drink Foto: El Heraldo

Chorote

A thicker, chocolate-heavy variation of pozol, where toasted corn kernels and fermented cacao beans are ground together to create a deep, chocolatey, texturized beverage.

🌽 Toasted Corn 🍫 Extra Cacao 🧊 Refreshing
📍 Traditional Markets

Dulce de Oreja de Mico

A classic regional sweet dessert made with a tiny variety of wild papaya. Simmered in dark cane sugar (piloncillo) syrup until it reaches a soft, chewy texture.

🍯 Cane Syrup 🍈 Wild Papaya 🍬 Sweet Treat

Tips for the Culinary Traveler

Make the most of your culinary adventure through the flavors of Tabasco.

🌶️
Respect the Amashito

The tiny amashito chili is a staple on every Tabasco table. It looks small and harmless, but it packs a powerful heat. Test a tiny drop first!

🧉
Unsweetened Pozol

Authentic pozol is traditionally unsweetened. It is consumed cold to keep you hydrated in the humid weather. Ask for sugar if you prefer a sweeter drink.

🐟
Fresh Catch Palapas

For the best pejelagarto and seafood, head to the coastal palapas in Paraíso or Frontera, where dishes are prepared right next to the river banks.

🍫
Cacao Hacienda Tours

Comalcalco is the heart of Mexican cacao. Take a guided tour of a cacao hacienda to taste raw cacao seeds fresh from the pod and learn chocolate history.

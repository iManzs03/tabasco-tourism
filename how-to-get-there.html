Getting Around

Local Transport in Tabasco

Once you arrive, use these reliable local options to travel between Villahermosa, Comalcalco, Paraíso, and other World Cup tourist sites.

Foto: El Heraldo
🚕

Official Taxis

In Villahermosa, use authorized "Radio Taxis" or airport taxi stands. Always confirm the fare zone before departing, as meters are not used.

Foto: El Heraldo
🚐

Colectivos / Combis

Inter-municipal passenger vans are cheap and frequent. They leave regularly from terminals in Villahermosa to Paraíso and Comalcalco.

Foto: Unsplash
🚗

Car Rentals

Available at Villahermosa Airport (VSA) through major brands. Highly recommended if you want to explore the Cacao Route or Centla swamps at your own pace.

Crucial Tips

Transportation Tips for Tourists

Ensure a safe and comfortable trip across the state during the World Cup matches.

🛑
Use Toll Roads

When driving, always choose toll roads ("Cuota") instead of free highways ("Libre") for better security, lighting, and road conditions.

🎫
Book ADO Online

ADO bus tickets sell out quickly during major events. Download the ADO app to book in advance and secure first-class seating.

📲
Offline Maps

Cellular coverage can be spotty in the jungle cacao routes and centla wetlands. Download offline maps (Google Maps) before traveling.

💵
Cash for Tolls

Highway tolls and local colectivos only accept Mexican Pesos (MXN) in cash. Keep at least $500 - $1000 MXN in smaller bills on hand.

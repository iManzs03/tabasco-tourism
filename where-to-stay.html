Helpful Advice

Tips for Staying in Tabasco

Make the most of your trip to this hidden gem of Mexico.

🌡️
Beat the Heat

Tabasco is tropical and humid. Book hotels with AC and an indoor pool — essential comfort during the hot summer months.

📅
Book Early!

Popular travel seasons bring many visitors. Secure your accommodation in advance, especially in Villahermosa.

🚌
Location Matters

Stay in Villahermosa for easy access to the whole state. Buses, colectivos, and taxis connect the capital to most tourist attractions within 2 hours.

💵
Currency Tips

Most hotels accept credit cards, but budget spots may be cash-only. Exchange USD at the airport or major banks for the best rates. Keep small pesos handy.
